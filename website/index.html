TextHalo for Mac: Listen, Dictate, and Assistant

Listen to selected text with Apple, Kokoro, Chatterbox, or hosted Fish Audio voices. Dictate on your Mac with macOS speech recognition or Whisper. Ask Assistant to write, edit, or read aloud using Apple Intelligence where available or TextHalo Cloud.

Local features are free. Cloud Assistant and hosted voices require sign-in, consent, and credits. Requires Apple Silicon; on-device Assistant requires macOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence.
