TextHalo for Mac: listen to selected text aloud

TextHalo is a free, open-source macOS menu bar app that reads selected text aloud with Apple system voices, Kokoro, and Chatterbox. Speech synthesis runs locally on your Mac.

Select a passage in the app you are using, press Command–Shift–S, and listen while you continue working. Choose the voice engine and capture method that suit your setup.
