# TextHalo 0.1.18

- Listen to Kindle ebooks with TextHalo Reader for Chrome on read.amazon.com and read.amazon.ca. Start at the open page or prepare a complete audiobook with local Kokoro narration.
- Play completed chapters while the rest of the audiobook is prepared. New chapters appear in the library and floating player as they finish, with chapter navigation and saved listening progress.
- After the complete transcript is captured, prepare more chapters without the reader connection. Saved audio plays offline.
- See the extension connection status in Audiobooks, with a reconnect hint when disconnected.
- Improved recognition of Roman front-matter page numbers and chapter headings affected by OCR spacing and line breaks. Existing chapter markers are repaired without regenerating audio.
- Clear outdated connection warnings after reconnecting and show the available chapter audio on the playback timeline.
- Updated the website with Kindle setup and listening information in all supported languages.

This release uses a replacement updater signing key. Install 0.1.18 manually from the website once to receive future automatic updates. Earlier installations retain their original update feed because its private key was lost with the erased release Mac.

Apple Silicon Macs only, macOS 14 or later. On-device Assistant requires supported Apple Intelligence hardware and macOS 26 or later.
