# TextHalo 0.1.21

- Export completed chapters and complete audiobooks as WAV audio files.
- Share audiobook audio using the macOS share menu, including AirDrop and Mail.
- Read detected chapter titles separately with a 0.75-second pause before the body in newly generated narration. Existing recordings retain their original audio.
- Detect when the Mac app has been uninstalled even if its browser helper remains, and offer an app download in TextHalo Reader 0.5.2.

Apple Silicon Macs only, macOS 14 or later. On-device Assistant requires supported Apple Intelligence hardware and macOS 26 or later.
